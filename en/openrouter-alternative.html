Do not apply 600+ to Ofox, and do not apply 138 to OpenRouter. Both catalogs can change on the same day.

Payment and pricing

Each fee is quoted from that vendor’s own page. This page does not subtract them.
ItemOfox public pagesOpenRouter pricing page
How billing is described The pricing doc, which says “Last updated on November 23, 2026,” describes pure pay-as-you-go: no subscriptions, no plans, no monthly fees, no minimum spend; top up first; balance does not expire; text models are billed per input and output token. Standard and Business are labeled pay-as-you-go. The FAQ says inference is billed at the provider’s list price, and the platform fee is charged when you buy credits, not on each request. Standard and Business have no minimum and no lock-in.
Platform fee Changelog v1.0.0 says “Pay-as-you-go — The model provider’s list price. Zero platform fee.” v1.0.54 says “0% platform fee — pay the model provider’s list price.” That is Ofox’s wording. This site has not checked an invoice. The FAQ says the fee on credit purchases is 5.5% on Standard and 8% on Business, and it is not charged per request. Free lists platform fees as N/A. Enterprise says fee discounts are available and pricing is set by contract.
Rebates or allowances v1.0.54 lists monthly spend rebates: 3% from $1,000, 4% from $5,000, 5% from $10,000, and 7% from $20,000. The same entry says a partner referral can add $3 of credit on the first top-up, recorded by Ofox. Those are their published rules. This site has not checked that a credit arrived. The FAQ says BYOK on Standard and Business includes $25,000 of list-price inference per month with no OpenRouter fee, then 5% of list price after that, deducted from credits. Enterprise allowances are listed as custom.
How you pay v1.0.54 names Airwallex and Stripe, and lists settlement currencies USD, CNY, and SGD. v1.0.0 names Stripe card top-ups. Whether a specific card works is not verified. The FAQ says Standard and Business accept credit and debit cards, AliPay, and crypto (USDC). Enterprise uses invoicing and purchase orders. They say prices exclude applicable tax.

Ofox changelog v1.0.39 compares its fees with OpenRouter in Ofox’s own words. This page does not repeat the savings figure from that entry, because this site did not calculate it from both catalogs.

5.5% and 8% are the platform fees OpenRouter publishes on credit purchases. Ofox publishes a 0% platform fee and provider list price. Those charges are not the same line item. This page does not subtract them into a savings amount. Cache discounts, batch pricing, tax, and the amount actually debited are not verified.
