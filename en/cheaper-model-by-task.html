Compare by task, not by an advertised lower rate

A lower bill means the same job still produces a usable result, and the arithmetic on rates you copied is lower. This page does not rank models, does not state a savings percent, and does not say one provider is cheaper. Whether Ofox is under a lab’s list price is a same-model, same-volume check, not a conclusion you can read here.

Five steps, on your own sample

  1. Write the task in one sentence. Examples: extract fields, rewrite, translate, edit a piece of code, or answer one question from a long document.
  2. Pick two ids that are actually in the catalog. Do not pick from memory. How to read them: the model list. Both ids have to be offered on the endpoint you really call.
  3. Send the same short input once to each. You decide whether the failure is acceptable: bad fields, a missed instruction, or code you cannot use. This site has no score.
  4. Open the provider’s current pricing page and copy the input and output rates for those two ids, per million tokens. If cache, batch, or long context is priced separately, keep it separate. Do not fold it into one “cheaper”.
  5. Enter monthly tokens in the monthly calculator. It can prefill one scenario from the dated Ofox public catalog. The other scenario, and any official lab rate, stay blank until you type them. Empty rates produce no total. The difference is subtraction, not a savings percent.
