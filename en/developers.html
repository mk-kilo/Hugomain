Cheaper is not the only reason to use one endpoint

List price is Ofox’s claim

Ofox says mainstream models are billed at the provider’s published list price and that the platform fee is 0%. That is their statement, not our guarantee. A direct enterprise discount from the lab can still be better. Ofox enterprise page · changelog, reviewed 2026-10-29.

Fewer places to configure

One provider can save setup time if you already use several SDKs and CLIs. Compatibility still has to be tested. Codex CLI and Claude Code are not verified on this site.

How to choose

One model for the long haul, and you want the lab’s own support: compare the official API first. You switch among GPT, Claude and Gemini often: compare Ofox on convenience and the rate you can actually see.

BEFORE YOU SIGN UP

Questions people ask first

Do I need a JXZNE account?

No. JXZNE is a free guide. When you want an API, you register at Ofox. The account, the balance and the key are on their side.

Does a ChatGPT or Claude subscription count as API credit?

Usually no. Chat subscriptions and API usage are billed separately, and a third-party balance is a third account. Check the plan you are actually buying.

Can I test the config without adding credit?

You can read the steps. A real model call usually needs a balance. Promotions and free credit follow Ofox’s current page. We do not promise a call with a zero balance.

Can I point Cursor straight at a third-party API?

Not every Cursor feature accepts a custom API. Ofox says direct Cursor integration is unsuitable. A configured CLI inside Cursor’s terminal is the path they describe. It still depends on the client version.

Will a specific card or network work?

Payment methods, countries and access limits change. Use the provider’s checkout and your local rules. This site cannot promise that a given card or network succeeds.

Why doesn’t this site ask for an API key?

A key is a credential. It belongs at the provider, on your machine, or in a backend you trust. Do not paste a live key into JXZNE or into any form you do not trust.
