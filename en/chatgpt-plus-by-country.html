How to read a country sample

  1. Open the price matrix, choose ChatGPT, then Plus. Compare the same plan and the same channel.
  2. Read the observation date and the source link. A row is a historical record, not today’s checkout and not an API rate.
  3. The USD column is a converted observation. Local currency, tax, and the payment method change what you pay.
  4. A missing country is a missing sample. Blank does not mean free, and it does not mean more expensive.

Questions

Is Plus $20 in every country?

The help center lists $20 a month. The product page also says list prices exclude local tax, and that checkout, store channel, and local-currency bills can differ. Use the dated matrix for country samples. Do not treat $20 as every store’s receipt.

Is the lowest converted row the cheapest country today?

No. It is one historical observation. Channel, tax, exchange rates, and account region change. This site does not rank countries by that low, and it does not suggest buying an account in another region.

Does Plus count as API credit?

The product page says Plus does not include API usage. API calls need a developer bill, or a usage account such as Ofox where you check that the model is actually listed. Do not add the two bills and call the sum the hidden price of Plus.
