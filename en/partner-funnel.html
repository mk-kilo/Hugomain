Ofox Partner tiers

The figures are from the Ofox Partner page, accessed 2026-11-26. Commission is calculated on referred users’ API spend, not on the top-up amount. A user who signs up through the referral link stays bound for life.

TierReferred users’ API spend that monthCommission
T1Default. Monthly spend under $10,0003%
T2Monthly spend reaches $10,0005%
T3Monthly spend reaches $100,0007%
T4Monthly spend reaches $300,0009%
  • Settlement is monthly. The minimum withdrawal is $50.
  • When monthly referral spend reaches a higher tier, that whole month’s commission is calculated at the new rate.
  • The current tier is kept for the following month. A downgrade happens only after two consecutive months below the current tier’s threshold.
  • The $3 bonus on a referred user’s first top-up is paid by Ofox. It is not taken out of commission.

Where the numbers come from

Read referred users, API spend, and commission in the Ofox Partner Dashboard. If first top-up or month-to-month retention is not on that screen, ask channel support how they count it. This page does not read Ofox’s private data.
