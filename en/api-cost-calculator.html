Questions

Why are the rates blank?

Rates start blank. After you pick a model, scenario A is filled from that model’s input and output rate in ofox-public-prices.json, and the page shows the file’s refresh time. Official lab rates are not filled in. The example button fills token counts only.

Is the difference a savings percent?

No. The difference is subtraction of the two monthly totals, in the same unit as the rates you typed. It excludes cache, batch, tax, and top-up fees, and it is not a measured saving.
